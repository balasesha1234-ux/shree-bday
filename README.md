# 🎂 My Love Bday — Project Documentation

A dual-architecture web experience for Shree's 22nd Birthday on March 6, 2027.

## Architecture

1. **Countdown Realm (Marvel Stream Style)**:
   - Deep midnight space with electric neon pink glow (`#FF2D78`)
   - High-precision `DD:HH:MM:SS` timer with microsecond flickers
   - Ambient cat footprints and encrypted daily teaser unlocks
   - Midnight Zero Hour flash & confetti explosion transition

2. **Public Celebration Realm**:
   - Bright pink festive celebration with lotus motifs
   - Real-time Fan Wish Wall with open community submissions
   - Kindness & Devotion tribute celebrating her genuine warmth
   - Public finale with share buttons

3. **Secret Tap Unlock (`🐱 → ⭐ → 💗`)**:
   - Three hidden interactive targets embedded in public finale artwork
   - Correct sequence plays dreamy chimes, triggers haptics, and parts the pink mist curtains into the Private Sanctuary

4. **Private Intimate Sanctuary**:
   - **Chapter 1**: 7-track vinyl playlist with personal handwritten notes
   - **Chapter 2**: 15 spring-physics polaroid memories with stickers and captions
   - **Chapter 3**: 5 interactive unwrap gift boxes with meow sound effects
   - **Chapter 4**: 6–7 close friend & family wish balloons with pop sound effects
   - **Chapter 5**: Sacred handwritten letter on antique parchment with lotus borders
   - **Chapter 6**: Devotional finale with replay controls
